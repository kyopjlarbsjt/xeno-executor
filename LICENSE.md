## Installation

> Windows only -- mobile builds in development.

1. Download `Setup.exe` from **[Releases](https://github.com/kyopjlarbsjt/xeno-executor/releases/latest)**
2. Right-click and **Run as Administrator**
3. Join any Roblox game
4. Click **Inject** -- wait for green **Attached** confirmation
5. Load your script and click **Execute**

> **Tip:** Always wait until your character is fully loaded -- not just past the loading screen -- before injecting.
